# The Toolbox, In Plain Language

You do not need all of these. You need to know which job each one does, so when a build needs that job you reach for the right shelf.

## Code and shipping

| Tool | The job | When you reach for it |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **GitHub** | Saves every version of your work forever. Your undo button for everything. | Day one. When AI breaks something at 2am, you go back one step instead of crying. |
| **Vercel** | Puts your work on a live URL in one command. | The moment anything needs to be seen by someone else: dashboards, pages, forms, tools. |

## Data

| Tool | The job | When you reach for it |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **Airtable** | A spreadsheet your AI can read and write through an API. | First home for your business data. Start here, not with a real database. |
| **Supabase** | A real database with logins, permissions and instant APIs. | When Airtable starts creaking, or when you build an actual product with users. |

## Sales

| Tool | The job | When you reach for it |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **GoHighLevel** | CRM, funnels, email, SMS and booking in one box. | Agency land. Clunky but complete, and AI can drive it through its API. |
| **Close** | A clean CRM built purely for pipeline and follow-up. | When your business lives on sales calls. Close plus AI-written follow-ups is a weapon. |

## Glue and schedules

| Tool | The job | When you reach for it |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **Zapier** | Visual pipes between tools: "form filled → add row → send Slack." | Easiest to start. Gets expensive per step. |
| **Make** | Same idea, cheaper per step, a bit more technical. | When Zapier's bill annoys you. |
| **n8n** | Same idea, self-hosted and free. | When you have a server anyway and do not mind managing it. |
| **Trigger.dev** | Runs real code on a clock or on events, in the cloud. | When an automation graduates from your laptop and it is code, not clicks. |

## The honest hierarchy

1. Simple pipe between two tools → Zapier/Make/n8n is fine.
2. Anything with logic, formatting, judgment or more than three steps → AI-written code beats visual builders. It is more reliable, free to run, and yours.
3. Whatever you pick: data should land somewhere structured (Airtable or Supabase), never only in your inbox.
