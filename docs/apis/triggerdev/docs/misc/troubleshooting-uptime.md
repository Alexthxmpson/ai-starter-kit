# Uptime Status

## Overview

This page describes a notification service for Trigger.dev platform incidents. Users can subscribe to receive email alerts when the platform experiences issues.

## How to Stay Informed

To receive email notifications about platform incidents—including creation, updates, and resolution—visit the status page and subscribe at [https://status.trigger.dev/](https://status.trigger.dev/).
